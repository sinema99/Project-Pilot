Internals Blockout Plan

Pilot drops into the city's industrial guts through a service hatch and works his way down through a fan hall, vents, control rooms, a turbine hall and coolant works. He finds the double jump about three quarters of the way in, climbs back through the turbine hall to the ledges he couldn't reach, then takes the press shaft down to the exit. Every piece has an id. Ask for changes by id ("T2 up 2 m", "make T0 20 m wider").

Section: the route unrolled

The critical path laid out flat: distance travelled along the bottom, height up the side. Cyan stretches need the double jump. The climb back up after the vault is the only time the mission goes uphill for long.

Design numbers

Before the double jumpLedges up to 4.0 m (hard limit 4.9 with mantle). Gaps up to 7 m (hard limit 9 at sprint). Drops are free: no fall damage, only kill volumes.
Double jump gatesLedges of 5.5–6 m (out of reach at 4.9 without it, comfortable at 7.9 with it). Gaps of 10–12 m.
Teases for later maps12 m+ gaps and 15 m+ heights. They should look nearly reachable and stay closed this mission (air dash, wall-run).
Moves on this mapWalk, run, sprint, jump, crouch, slide, mantle, vault. The double jump unlocks in the Capacitor Vault. Wall-run, wall climb, dash and rail grind are off.
