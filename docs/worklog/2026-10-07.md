## Firing range

Spec: [specs/firing-range.md](../specs/firing-range.md). Grilled, specced and built the same day.

- A 50 x 50 m slab at world (0, 0, -150) with a causeway out of its +Z edge. A box dummy sleeps until
  pilot steps on, then circles him at 14 m and fires a travelling laser bolt every 2-3 s with a charge-up
  flash. It cannot die and cannot hurt him: a hit on pilot is a mesh flash, sparks, camera shake and a red
  vignette; a hit on the dummy is a flash, sparks, a wobble and a floating damage number.
- Reflected bolts hurt the dummy through `take_hit(amount, kind, point)`, the hook for future attacks. A
  perfect reflect (50) now homes onto the strafing dummy; a normal one (20) is a random throw in the cone.
- Corner HUD on the slab (damage, hits, hits taken, reflects, DPS). F9 reset, F10 fire, F11 freeze.
- New: `scripts/range/` (firing_range, range_dummy, range_bolt, range_stats, pilot_hit_feedback),
  `scenes/firing_range.tscn`, `tests/test_firing_range.gd`, `tests/_probe_range_shot.gd`. Edited:
  `pilot9.gd`, `pilot9_vfx.gd`, `trial.tscn`, `project.godot` (layer 9 `range_dummy`).
