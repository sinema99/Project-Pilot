## Reflect / parry — built

Spec: [specs/pilot9-reflect.md](../specs/pilot9-reflect.md), "Built". Left mouse (gamepad right
shoulder) with the staff drawn spins it in front of him for 0.6 s, then a 0.4 s recovery. Movement
stays free; mantle, vault, wall-run and rail grind refuse the press. Anything in the `reflectable`
group that touches the spin zone is turned: inside the first 0.15 s it goes back to its shooter
(a perfect, with a FOV kick and hit-stop); later it goes into a 60 degree cone around the camera aim.
Both arms are solved from the rig's rest pose, the staff and arm blend share one weight, and two
fading trails draw the disc. Test rig: F7 fires a ball from a post at z = 10 in `trial.tscn`, F8 toggles
auto-fire. New: `reflect_spin`, `reflect_projectile`, `reflect_test_emitter`,
`tests/test_pilot9_reflect.gd`, `tests/_probe_reflect_shot.gd`. Edited: `pilot9.gd`, `pilot9_vfx.gd`,
`project.godot`, `trial.tscn`. Full suite green (858 tests). The probe screenshots and a timed
perfect and normal reflect both looked right. Not yet played: the feel and the timings.

## Pipe hall — built

Spec: [specs/pipe-hall.md](../specs/pipe-hall.md). A 96 x 56 x 46 m dark industrial hall built from
a reference painting the user gave, placed at world (-99, 0, 84) where they circled the empty area
past the jump grid's +Z edge, joined to the slab by a 32 m causeway. Three levels of catwalk and
ramps, giant pipes (a floor pipe you can run across, two elbows, wall-run risers), a long grind
rail from the east platform to the central span, cool spotlights with shafts, warm caged lamps, and
a local ReflectionProbe ambient for the darkness. All generated by `scripts/pipe_hall.gd` with no
owners, so `trial.tscn` only gained one instanced node. New: `pipe_hall.gd`, `pipe_hall.tscn`,
`tests/test_pipe_hall.gd`, `tests/_probe_hall_shot.gd`. Edited: `trial.tscn`. Full suite green (863
tests). Probe screenshots from five vantage points looked right. Not yet played: whether the routes
flow, the ramp slopes, and the wall-run on the cylinder risers.

## Mead habitat — built

Spec: [specs/mead-habitat.md](../specs/mead-habitat.md). A blockout of the user's Syd Mead painting
(space-station cylinder interior) to judge the look before they model it in Blender: ribbed white
vault, brown tower with a flared base, dark rock bridge into it, lake, terraces, hydroponic beds,
corn, a control cab on a walkway, backdrop out to about 1 km. Generated by `scripts/habitat.gd` with
no owners, placed at world (330, -16, 0) with a 160 m causeway toward the main area (it stops about
40 m short of the existing floor). Tuned to the `CabVantage` marker by comparing probe screenshots
with the painting; no people, water has no collider over a solid bed. New: `habitat.gd`,
`habitat.tscn`, `shaders/habitat_water.gdshader`, `tests/test_habitat.gd`,
`tests/_probe_habitat_shot.gd`. Edited: `trial.tscn`. Full suite green (870 tests). Not yet played.
