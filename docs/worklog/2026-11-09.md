## Knight boss body

The user sent a drawing (an armoured, robed knight floating, spiked pauldrons, hood-helm with a cream
mask, sash, clawed gauntlets, pointed sabatons; red swords ignored) and asked for a new character on
pilot9's skeleton. Grilled 13 (spec `docs/specs/knight-boss.md`): Godot code, robe as 4 panels skinned
in code, pilot9's meshes removed, a BOSS, body only, in the Boss Arena frozen in the drawing's pose at
1 m hover, 1.5x, drawing colours through RETRO, both hands the same claw (the one non-recommendation),
spikes modelled + slots as decals, name Knight.

Built `scripts/boss/knight_body.gd` (`KnightBody`): pilot9.glb instanced, meshes and AnimationPlayer
freed, 7 skinned meshes (one per material) built from lofted rings, domes, cones and flat decals in
the T-pose rest space. Robe weights blend hips -> thighs -> near shin. Pose is a model-space table per
bone. F6 swaps to `idle.res`, sampled by bone name. `KnightBoss` node in `scenes/boss_arena.tscn`;
`BossArena` puts him on `BossAnchor` facing the spawn.

Correction: the drawing faces the viewer, so the reaching arm is his RIGHT. The grill summary said
left; the build matches the drawing.

Probe rounds (`tests/_probe_knight_shot.gd`, windowed): round 1 had a big round face-like mask, spikes
reading as fur and chest slots reading as tears; mask shrunk, spikes fewer/chunkier, chest slots
swapped for wavy ink lines on the band edges, rim ink on the pauldron lames, hood point leaned back.
The arena sun sits behind him from the spawn, so the player sees his shadow side: the default
shadow went greenish-grey, a full brown tint went burlap; settled on `shadow_tone` 0.66 + tint 0.8
(lit sage, soft olive-taupe shadow). Idle shot: robe panels follow the legs, no tearing.

Tests: `tests/test_knight_body.gd`, 7 tests, all pass.

## Knight boss moveset

The user: "the boss looks decent, lets get him moving". Grilled 10 (docs/specs/knight-moveset.md),
every recommendation taken: he floats (never walks), wakes at 40 m, drifts round pilot at ~18 m, and
picks laser / missiles / smash by distance every 2.5-4 s. Hits are feedback only (no HP either side).

Built:
- `KnightBody` pose layer: `solve_pose` (table -> local rotations), `show_blend(a, b, t, extra)`,
  `set_lean` about the hips, `bone_world`, `take_hit` (white overlay flash + red burst). The F6 idle
  swap and its clip are gone.
- `KnightPoses`: 13 key poses (drawing, hover, wake, 4 laser, 2 missile, 4 smash).
- `KnightBrain` (child of the knight, built by `BossArena`): states, float/drift/leash/lean/bob, the
  three moves as timed phases, pure rules (`move_weights`, `pick_move`, `beam_hits`,
  `smash_inner_hits`, `wave_hits`, `clamp_leash`), F7-F11 debug keys.
- `KnightLaser` (aim line / locked line / beam + muzzle), `KnightMissile` (Area3D, reflect contract,
  climb then capped-turn homing, smoke puffs), `KnightSmash` (ring + fill, shockwave, crack),
  `KnightFx` (colours, glow material, puff mesh, one-shot bursts gated by MOVEMENT EFFECTS).
- `PilotHitFeedback.shake(amount, seconds)` for the smash's near-miss shake.

Pose gotcha found in the probe: a HANGING arm (BACK +-80 first) swings forward about RIGHT, not UP -
rotating a down-pointing arm about Y does nothing. The first smash poses left his claws at hip height,
so pinning them to the floor sank him; the rebuilt dive/stuck poses pitch him forward 60-70 deg with
arms straight down (hips end 0.74 m over the floor). Smoke/burst quads read as black square chips in
the cel look; they are low-poly balls now.

Probe: `tests/_probe_knight_moves.gd` (windowed; pilot frozen 18 m out, each move stepped by hand,
close / side / pilot-eye shots). Laser draw->thrust->lock->beam+recoil, missile volley fan, smash
rise->ring->dive->stuck all read.

Tests: `tests/test_knight_moveset.gd` (27) + 3 new / 1 replaced in `test_knight_body.gd`; the two
knight files pass together (38 tests, 470 checks). Full suite: 1439 tests, 36234 checks, only
2 failed checks in `test_ichi_parked_ship` (they fail the same with the knight tests removed, so not
from this pass). The suite now takes ~600 s: a 590 s `timeout` kills it before the summary.
Not yet played, uncommitted.

## Knight coat moves (docs/specs/knight-coat.md)

User after playing the moveset: "the new boss animations look good ... the biggest blaring problem
i see is his coat skirt. it needs to be moving." Grilled 5 (idle + reactive, coat bones + springs,
~5-8 cm idle / ~20 deg drift / 40 deg cap, sweep moves into the bones, sash tails too), all recs.

- `KnightBody` adds 18 bones in code before the Skin is built: `CoatU0-7` / `CoatL0-7` (a two-bone
  chain down each pleat ridge, waist -> knee row -> hem) and `SashTail0/1`. The robe, its inside and
  its crease ink weigh on the two nearest chains (Hips holds the band under the sash); the tails
  blend Hips -> their bone. `show_pose` / `show_blend` stop at `coat_first`.
- The robe mesh is built straight; the drawing's sweep is the chains' dormant target
  (`coat_sweep`, 1 dormant -> 0 over the 1.5 s wake; the brain sets the target, `reset()` snaps it).
  The old hips->thigh->shin weighting lives on as each chain's leg target (a third of the thigh at
  the knee row, half at the hem with 0.4 of the near shin).
- `tick_coat()` (in `_physics_process`; probes call it by hand): hips world velocity / acceleration
  / yaw rate, in the hips frame -> per-segment 2D tilt springs (1.1 Hz lower, 1.3x upper, 1.7x tails,
  damping 0.3) pulled to a rest shifted by drag 8.5 deg per m/s, kick 1 deg per m/s^2, swirl,
  30% gravity, plus a travelling idle billow (1 deg, 3 s). Soft cap 40 deg, 5 deg inward cap, a
  jump over 5 m resets the motion. Tunables: KnightBody export group "Coat".
- Measured: idle hem travel ~6.7 cm (world), 4 m/s drift trails the hem ~20 deg, one overshoot then
  settles, a 47 m/s dive flares the hem >25 deg without passing either cap.
- Probes: the dormant front shot matches knight-boss-r2.png's swept silhouette; laser recoil flares
  the hem, the smash impact flings it out behind him.
- `tests/run_tests.gd`: the filter now matches "file::method", so `-- test_knight_` runs both
  knight files (~25 s) instead of the 10 min suite.

Tests: 7 coat tests replace the old hips->legs weighting test; knight files 45 tests, 575 checks
pass. Full suite not re-run (it outlasts the 590 s timeout). Not yet played, uncommitted.
