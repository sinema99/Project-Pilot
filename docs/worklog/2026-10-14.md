  eye is a shaded pit (lit / dark inner wall face, slate floor, black eyewall shadow). Eye spiral removed.
- Lightning: cyan glow + white core lighting the cloud's own shape, jagged crawler veins on bright flashes,
  2.5 / s from frame 0 building to 6 / s, sting kept.
- `scripts/cutscene/intro_space.gd` (`wall_spin_rate`, `band_spin_rate`, `storm_spin_at`) and
  `intro_space_lightning.gd` (layer-aware spots).
- Tests: two cutscene tests rewritten. Full suite: 1100 tests, 2 checks fail, both in trial.tscn and from
  before this work (`ApplyCelMech` EXIA target, two current cameras).
- Probe-checked frames at 0.5 / 1.3 / 2.3 / 3 / 11.8 s. Not yet played.
