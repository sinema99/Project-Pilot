## Jetpack: Airframe Ultra look, from scratch

The user loves the look of Airframe Ultra (Videocult) and wants the game to look like it; they're
redoing pilot / mech / ship low-poly in Blender themselves. Asked for research plus a clean test
scene called Jetpack: pilot, flat ground, boxes.

Research: Videocult has published nothing on the rendering, and press only says "smeary,
low-poly", "jittering textures". So I read the Steam screenshots at 1920x1080, 2x zoom:
- geometry is drawn at FULL resolution (clean edges), not a low-res render;
- the PS1 read is small, grungy, photo textures drawn unfiltered (hard texel blocks on a sharp
  screen);
- no outlines, no cel bands, hard sun shadows, a saturated grade;
- fine vertical CRT stripes plus a slight colour fringe;
- chunky square-pixel effects.

Grilled 10, every recommendation taken (spec `docs/specs/jetpack-look.md`):
- no cel/ink in Jetpack;
- textures generated in code;
- 32 texels per metre as the project rule;
- vertex snap on (240 grid at full res), affine built but off;
- per-vertex lighting with a per-pixel hard shadow;
- stripes, fringe and grade, but no dither or bars;
- full pilot9 with the extras off;
- 200 m slab + 14 boxes + blue sky, fog 60-150 m;
- QA keys 5-9 and a corner label.

Built:
- `shaders/ps1/ps1_surface.gdshader`:
  - Gouraud lambert computed in vertex from a `sun_dir` uniform; light() takes the sun's
    ATTENUATION, so the shadow stays per pixel.
  - Clip-space snap, skipped on orthographic (shadow) passes.
  - Affine by the uv*w / w trick.
  - World mapping with one axis per face.
  - `cull_disabled`, because his GLB materials are double-sided.
- `shaders/ps1/ps1_screen.gdshader`: canvas_item over the screen texture.
- `scripts/rendering/ps1_textures.gd` (`Ps1Textures`): ground, concrete, rust, paint_blue and
  paint_red. Seamless FastNoiseLite plus random-walk cracks/drips, seeded.
- `scripts/rendering/jetpack_look.gd` (`JetpackLook`):
  - Finds `ps1_surface` meta meshes under World and swaps the pilot's skinned-body surfaces
    (his albedo colours carried over).
  - The filter CanvasLayer sits at layer -1, under the HUD.
  - Directional shadows are set to HARD while it lives and restored on exit.
  - Look-off uses StandardMaterial3D world triplanar with the same textures.
- `Jetpack.tscn` at the root, generated by a throwaway Python script; boxes are hand-editable
  nodes with metadata.
- pilot9: new `movement_effects_allowed` export (Movement Effects group), which gates the VFX
  and afterimages. Jetpack sets it false, `starting_item = null`, and rim light false.

Probe (`tests/_probe_jetpack_shot.gd`, windowed, 5 states): the first pass was flat (ambient 0.9
swamped the sun), the paint was electric and the concrete read as granite. Fixes:
- ambient 0.55 + sun 1.6;
- paint muted and chips rarer;
- speckle cut;
- filter saturation 1.35 -> 1.2.

Affine visibly wiggles the ground cracks per 2 m cell.

Tests: `tests/test_jetpack_look.gd`, 5 tests / 45 checks, pass.

Not yet played, uncommitted. Open: chunky-pixel jetpack/trail effects, the user's low-poly
models, no kill plane at the slab edge (100 m out, hidden in fog).

## Jetpack pilot body

The user's new Mixamo-rigged low-poly body (`Jog Forward.fbx`, mesh only) now replaces pilot9's
body in Jetpack and plays all of his clips. Spec: `docs/specs/jetpack-pilot-body.md`.

## Jetpack texture pass 2: colour pixels

Grilled (8 questions, every recommendation taken), then built. Spec: `docs/specs/jetpack-look.md`
"Texture pass 2". The photo-grime textures read as fine noise, so they are gone. Every surface in
Jetpack is now its own material colour cut into Minecraft-style pixels: 16 texels per metre (new
project rule, the 8 key flips 16 <-> 8), clumps of 2-4 texels, and 4 stepped shades (-15 % to
+10 %, brightness only) with a few lone texels one shade off.

- `shaders/ps1/ps1_surface.gdshader`: no texture sampler. Integer-hash cell noise in texel space,
  projected along the normal's dominant axis. `mapping` 0 world (the level), 1 object, 2 rest pose.
  Texels smaller than a screen pixel settle to the mean shade, so the far ground doesn't shimmer.
- `scripts/rendering/jetpack_look.gd`: every MeshInstance3D under World is picked up, no tags. The
  pilot's skinned body is rebuilt once with its rest positions/normals in CUSTOM0/CUSTOM1
  (`bake_rest_pose`), so the pixels ride the body. `object_scale` comes from the skin (the
  jetpack_pilot.fbx body is stored at 1/100, so it is 100). New exports: shade_count, shade_dark,
  shade_light, clump_texels, jitter_chance.
- `scripts/rendering/ps1_textures.gd` deleted. `Jetpack.tscn` boxes have plain colour materials
  in place of the `ps1_surface` tags.
- Tests: `tests/test_jetpack_look.gd` rewritten (6 tests, including the rest-pose bake and own
  colours). Full suite: only the 2 Ichi checks already failing above. Probe shots
  (`tests/_probe_jetpack_shot.gd`, now 16/8) show the clumps on every box and the ground.

Not yet played, uncommitted. The current body is plain white, so its pixels only show in its
shading.

## Jetpack body corrective (feet, head, hands)

Grilled (5 questions, every recommendation taken), then built. Spec: `docs/specs/jetpack-body-corrective.md`.

- `scripts/body_corrective.gd` (new) is a SkeletonModifier3D. It is built only when
  `body_fit_skeleton` ran, so only Jetpack has it, and the 0 key toggles it (jetpack_look label).
  - **Feet:** his Foot/Toes keys are dropped. The foot sits at 90 deg to the shin and points where
    the knee points. Within about 10 cm of the ground (found by a raycast) it lies flat on the
    ground normal; in the air it takes a 20 deg point. The toes bend up when the tip would sink.
  - **Head:** `chin_down_deg` 25 levels the modelled chin-up face. The value came from a 0/18/35
    sweep of side-on head shots. The neck and head are rebuilt from the chest (0.5 of its yaw,
    0.35 of its lean) plus a clamped camera look. The look fades out in mantle, vault, wall climb
    and wall run.
  - **Hands:** the thumb and index curl by a fist amount read from his clips' index curl, using
    dummy bones that catch the humanoid finger tracks. Clips with no finger keys sit at a relaxed
    0.35; anchored moves and rails grip at 0.8. The wrist is clamped to 40 deg. In mantle, vault
    and wall climb, a two-bone arm IK reaches each hand to where HIS longer arm would have put it.
- `pilot9.gd`: the body's finger bones and the clip dummies are added to his skeleton
  (`_add_body_bones`). In the fit, leaf and added bones now inherit their parent's turn, so the
  hand follows the forearm and the wrist kink is gone. The fit's turns and his original
  lengths/motion_scale are kept for the corrective.
- Probe `tests/_probe_jetpack_body.gd` now takes `off` and `chin=N` and adds side, head and feet
  shots. Before/after: the run's -80 deg foot flips are gone, the feet plant flat, the face is
  level and the run makes fists.
- Tests: `tests/test_jetpack_body_corrective.gd` (4 new). All `test_jetpack*` pass (14 tests /
  116 checks), and so do all `test_pilot9*` (732 tests / 17752 checks) and afterimage. The full
  suite didn't finish inside my 590 s window, so I haven't seen its result.

Not yet played, uncommitted. Torso was checked in shots and left alone.

## Jetpack body corrective, round 2, built

Built `docs/specs/jetpack-body-corrective-r2.md` (see its "Built" section). `scripts/body_corrective.gd`:

- Feet take their heading from his facing (the body's +Z plus 0.35 of the hips' yaw) plus 8 deg
  of toe-out, never from the knee. Air pitch is capped at 45 deg toe-down.
- The face is level. `FACE_HIS` was measured from the head mesh and leans back 25.8 deg, so
  `chin_down_deg` is 25.8. `lean_follow` is 0, the look is yaw only, and `head_turn_max_deg` is 90.
- The hands hold a loose fist by default and a full fist when he runs, sprints, mantles, vaults,
  wall-climbs or rail-grinds. Clip finger data is no longer read.

- foot yaw stays within ±14.3 deg;

- the idle index curls 90 deg.
