# 2026-11-04

## Boss arena rework: bare floor, taller ranges, overcast, scraped pad

The user opened `boss1.tscn`: things clipping through the floor, mountains hard to see, the whole
scene blindingly white. Grilled 4 (spec `docs/specs/boss-arena.md`, "Rework") and built.

The clipping was the scrub every cap grows, now off on RightWide in the arena only (`bare_caps`).
The ranges stand 2x taller in the backdrop bake only (`range_lift`). The arena went from the shot's
clear day to an overcast, one `BossArena.OVERCAST` table feeding the arena and the bake. The floor is
a painted wind-scraped pad, 30% deck, from `tools/gen_boss_floor.gd`, on a visual-only surface.

Near-white at the spawn went from 38% of the frame to 0%. The floor measured pure 255 white before.

Wrong turns: a woven grid on the floor looked like brush grain, then shadow acne, then film grain.
It was S3TC on the floor image, which also caused the swinging greys; the same on the backdrop made
the sky's coloured arcs. Both are BPTC now, and the two guessed fixes were taken back out. PIXEL's
haze hid the whole world at this scale, not caused by this work; overridden in the arena scene only.

Cutscene untouched: the new exports default off. Not yet played, uncommitted.

## COMPOSITE look: the cutscene composite in live play

The user asked whether the cutscene composite only works on cutscenes and still cameras. It does not:
both effects run per frame on any camera, and CinemaPost was already live through RETRO. DepthComposite
costs about 0.27 ms on the 3060 at 1440p. Grilled 7 decisions (docs/specs/live-composite.md), every
recommendation taken, then built.

LOOK is now RETRO / COMPOSITE on I; RETRO stays the default and is unchanged. COMPOSITE is RETRO plus
`[DepthComposite, composite_post.tres]`, with the glow on the map's real sun. CEL, PIXEL, PIXEL FILTER,
COLOUR DEPTH, PAINTED SHADING and CEL SCATTER left the menu and their QA keys went; the code stays.
Ichi is out. `internals` has its own dark values. New `DepthComposite.dark_keep_chroma` (0 on the shots),
because luminance cannot tell a red bolt from white armour.

Probed on trial, starter_map, internals and boss_arena, plus a running camera sweep and the pause
menu. Full suite: 1401 tests, only the 2 old test_ichi_parked_ship failures, plus the old exit segfault.
That run predates the last tuning edits; the affected test groups were re-run after them and pass.
Not yet played, uncommitted. Outline flicker in motion and bolts inside the band are unproven.
