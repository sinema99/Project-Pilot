## EXIA run: slower, upright, feet that come back

Spec: [specs/exia-sprint.md](../specs/exia-sprint.md), "Retune 2026-10-13". Grilled, then built the same day.
The ask: the mech leans too far forward, the run is too fast and the feet slide, and the feet look wrong
after moving.

- Found the "feet look wrong afterwards" bug: no exported clip has a track for `toe.L.001` (the left sole
  plate; the right one has a track), so after a run it stayed at the run's last angle. `mech.gd::hold_sprint_bones()`
  now gives every clip a one-key track holding the standing value for any bone the run moves and the clip doesn't.
- `scripts/mech_sprint.gd`: 14 m/s (was 20), 1.0 s cycle (was 0.8), 0.35 s contact (was 0.28), stride
  front 1.85 m (4.9 m floor path), lean 8 deg (was 20) with a 1 deg dip at the low point (was 2), bob 0.2 m (was 0.25).
- `scripts/mech.gd`: `SPRINT_SPEED` 14; walk <-> run crossfades cut to 0.15 s (`SPRINT_BLEND_TIME`)
  so two clips disagreeing about the feet only lasts a moment; bank full at 14 m/s on a 12 m circle.
- `tests/test_mech_sprint.gd` retuned, with three new tests (lean, every clip keys every bone the run
  moves, the left plate is back to standing after a run). Full suite: 1035 tests pass.
- Probe re-taken (`screenshots/mech-sprint/`). Not yet played.

## Staff draw / holster animation

Spec: [specs/pilot9-equip-holster.md](../specs/pilot9-equip-holster.md). Grilled, then built the same day.
The ask: `R` should stop snapping. He reaches back, grabs the staff, and it spins in his hand (not on
the press). Holster is the same thing backwards.

- `scripts/items/equip_anim.gd` (new, `EquipAnim` on pilot9): a 0.55 s procedural timeline. Reach 0.15 s
  (the arm eases into `hold_rotations` on its own modifier), swing 0.12 s plus spin 0.20 s as one
  counter-clockwise turn seen from behind, then settle 0.08 s. The turn is the swing plus one extra full
  turn, with the reflect's blur disc. Holster plays the timeline backwards.
- The staff is placed every frame from the live back placement, the live hand and the model's facing.
  The grab slides the grip and flattens the back's tilt, and the spin tilts the plane over to the
  trailing hold, so neither end pops (tested).
- Rules: ignored in mantle, vault and wall-run, and while one is already playing. Reflect is ignored
  throughout, because the staff only counts as drawn at the end. A blocked move starting mid-animation
  finishes it instantly.
- `item_holder.gd`: new `set_drawn()` and `drawn_arm_target()`. `pilot9.gd`: the `equip` input goes
  through `press_equip()`. `toggle_drawn()` stays the instant snap that scripts and tests use.
- `tests/test_pilot9_equip_holster.gd`: 14 new tests. Full suite: 1050 tests pass.
- Probe `tests/_probe_equip_shot.gd` (slow-motion draw and holster from behind). Only the idle case
  was shot; the running settle is judged in play.

  painted patches + chipped terminator. Old noise planet kept outside the crop.
- New `planet_earth.gdshaderinc` (shared cloud field), `planet_clouds.gdshader` (cel cloud shell with a storm
  comma on the ice front, Atlantic bands, Florida clear), `planet_halo.gdshader` (stepped atmosphere crescent).
- `intro_space.gd`: cloud drift from the shot time; sun direction into the halo.
- Tests: 6 new in `tests/test_cutscene_intro_space.gd`. Full suite 1091 of 1093 pass; the 2 failures are the
  known trial.tscn ones above. Probe frames checked at 0 / 7.15 / 12 s. 8.6 ms a frame at 1080p.
