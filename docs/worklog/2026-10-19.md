## Intro halo glows, mega city stops flickering

- `planet_halo.gdshader`: the three hard cel bands (two read as rings) are gone. One soft glow now:
  `core_strength` 1.3 (HDR, so the glow pass blooms it), exponential `falloff` 0.14, fades out by
  `outer_edge` 0.75, `inner_soft` fade-in at the surface. The night-side thinning is kept.
- City flicker: the city is all hard tests finer than a pixel (streets, lips, rails, towers), so it
  crawled as the camera moved. New `city_pixel()` takes 4 rotated-grid samples per pixel (step from
  dFdx/dFdy of the Earth direction, taken at the top of `fragment()`) and blends the city's colour,
  coverage and shadow over the ground. `city_aa` turns it off.
- City "fighting": 20 pairs of mega-city platforms overlap on purpose (the generator's stacked edges)
  but drew at one height, first in the list winning with no edge. The smaller one is on top now, with
  its own lip and a short shadow on the one under it (`city_stacked_on`).
- Render probe at shot 4 s checked all three. One run of the suite failed
  test_storm_swallows_the_vanishing_point_and_lightning_lifts once (not touched; passed on rerun);
  otherwise only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures. Not yet played, uncommitted.
