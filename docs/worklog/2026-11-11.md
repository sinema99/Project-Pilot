## pilot9: strafe locomotion back (S backpedals)

Grilled, specced (`docs/specs/pilot9-strafe-return.md`) and built. Undoes the 2026-09-07
turn-to-face for normal movement, so the DayZ free-look (Alt / R3) now has a job: his body and
movement hold while the camera swings.

- `face_travel_direction` now ships `false`. Walk, run, sprint and the air face camera-forward
  and play RC's directional clips; S is `run_backward` / `walk_backward`.
- `_faces_travel()` (crouch, tredge, or the flag) is the one rule. Slide, dash, mantle, wall-run,
  wall-climb and rail were already welded to their own heading; the vault gets its own branch
  (lerps to `_vault_dir`) because it used to fall through to face-travel.
- Sprint with any backward stick is a run, speed and clip both (`_sprint_direction_ok()`); the
  sprint toggle stays on, so letting go of S sprints again.
- `_travel_blend()` uses the body-frame formula in every mode. At steady state it matches stock
  RC's input feed, and while he is still turning back to camera-forward after a slide it does
  not play `run_backward` over a body facing the old way.
- Body lean: the scalar speed change is aimed along his travel (`_lean_travel_dir`) and split
  onto his body, so a backpedal start pitches back, braking a backpedal pitches forward and a
  strafe start rolls toward the strafe. A constant-speed carve still pitches nothing, and running
  forward uses the same maths as before.

Tests: `test_pilot9_turn_to_face` reworked around the new default (+6 tests), `test_pilot9_body_lean`
+4, and the dayz camera check flipped. Full suite green: 1477 tests, 36530 checks.
