## hub2 industrial texturing pass

User: texture the hub2 scene as an industrial place, darker edges and corners, edge wear, vents on
some walls, random decals. Grilled 11 (spec `docs/specs/hub2-industrial.md`) and built:

- Procedural world-space surface shader (`shaders/hub2_wear.gdshader`): walls / floors / ceilings
  treated differently, convex-edge chips, concave-corner grime, seams, rivets, streaks, rust low down.
- Offline bake (`tools/bake_hub2_wear.gd`, `scripts/hub2_wear_bake.gd`) writes per-face distances to the
  nearest edges / creases / piece-into-piece seams into the meshes (`assets/hub2_wear/`), and scatters
  ~11 300 decals (`scripts/hub2_decal_atlas.gd` draws the sheet in code: vents, hazard stripes, plates,
  pipes, stencil labels, streaks, rust, lamps with a glow layer). Visual only, no collision.
- `scripts/hub2_wear.gd` node in `scenes/hub2.tscn` swaps it all in; `ApplyCelGround` removed from the
  scene (its override would have covered the wear).
- First probe shots: far too blue (the scene's blue ambient) and a rust stripe in every corner; palette
  warmed to land on grey, rust cut back. The first bake produced 5.5 M verts (kilometre faces cut at
  6 m everywhere); now cut only near a line at 12 m, ~638 k.
- Tests: `tests/test_hub2_wear.gd` new; `tests/test_hub2.gd` updated (it still expected the first
  build's spawn at the corridor end; it now follows the spawn empty).
