## Cel scatter

Spec: [specs/cel-scatter.md](../specs/cel-scatter.md), grilled (8 decisions) and built from the
user's close-up of a worn pipe. The cel shader now chips the edge between its light and dark
bands: small hard-edged Voronoi flecks, dark ones pushed into the lit band about twice as far as
light ones into the dark. Surface-anchored (UV on characters, world on level), densest at the edge
and fading to nothing, applied to everything on `cel-shader-base`. CEL SCATTER row in graphics
settings, default ON, greyed in the PIXEL look. Painted shading, palette and band count are
untouched.

Tuned from windowed probe shots (`tests/_probe_scatter_shot.gd`): chips were too large at first, so
`scatter_world_size` 0.05 -> 0.03 and `scatter_uv_size` 0.025 -> 0.015. Cast-shadow edges only chip
where the shadow has a penumbra. Not yet played.

New: `shaders/cel/includes/scatter.gdshaderinc`, `tests/test_cel_scatter.gd`,
`tests/_probe_scatter_shot.gd`, `docs/specs/cel-scatter.md`.
Edited: `base-cel-shader.gdshaderinc`, `cel-shader-base.gdshader`, `project.godot` (6 `scatter_*`
globals), `scripts/settings.gd`, `scripts/ui/tabs/tab_settings.gd`.

## EXIA sprint

Spec: [specs/exia-sprint.md](../specs/exia-sprint.md), built in the spec's order. Toggle on `sprint`,
20 m/s, a Titanfall bounding run (2 steps/s, 10 m bounds).

- `scripts/mech_sprint.gd` builds `MECH_sprint` at boot (31 keys, 30 fps). Legs are solved, not
  keyed: each key places a ball-of-foot target (planted ball moves back at exactly 20 m/s through
  contact, Hermite swing arc in flight) and a two-bone solve fits thigh and shin. Authored in mech
  space so the armature's yaw and scale are absorbed. Tunables are consts at the top, by bone.
- `mech.gd`: toggle (pilot9's rules), 7 -> 20 in 0.5 s, 0.25 s brake, turn rate 10 -> 4 by speed, bank
  (<= 12 deg, composes with Model's yaw), phase-matched crossfades, FOV +10, camera bounce / footfall
  dip / shake (CAMERA MOTION), footfall dust (`scripts/mech_vfx.gd`, MOVEMENT EFFECTS).
- `Pilot9Vfx.dust_burst` and `_particles` lifted to statics so the mech reuses them without a copy.
- Tests: `tests/test_mech_sprint.gd` (clip + movement). Probe: `tests/_probe_mech_sprint.gd` writes the
  eight pose frames to `screenshots/mech-sprint/`.

Departures: forearm overshoot is a one-frame lag behind the upper arm (follow-through) rather than a
5 deg overshoot-and-settle. The rig's standing feet are 0.09 m asymmetric, so the mirror test allows
0.15 m. The sole floor is the standing ball height (0.16 m in this export, not the spec's 0.07).
Not yet played; the user corrects it pose by pose from the probe frames. One failing test,
`habitat: no generated child has an owner`, is unrelated (untouched habitat work).
