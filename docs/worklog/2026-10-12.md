# 2026-10-12

## EXIA sprint: grounded titan run

Spec: [specs/exia-sprint.md](../specs/exia-sprint.md), "Redo 2026-10-12". Grilled, then built the same day.
The ask: the legs look wonky and the arms are out of sync; make it run like a Titanfall titan or a
Pacific Rim Jaeger.

- Diagnosis, measured off the old clip: each foot was down only 0.15 s of the 1.0 s cycle (in the air
  ~70% of the time), the stride never went more than 1.2 m behind the hip (no push-off), the swing foot
  pawed back before landing, and the arms swung on a sine against legs that don't move on one.
- `scripts/mech_sprint.gd`: the gait half was rewritten (the sampler and clip writer are unchanged).
  0.8 s cycle, 2.5 steps/s, 0.28 s contact, 0.25 m bob, 0.15 m sway over the planted foot. The foot is
  now modelled as it is on the rig: a stilt (foot + toe) over a sole plate (`toe.*.001`). It lands on
  the plate's back edge, slaps flat, and pivots on the tip for the push-off. The arms are timed by the
  legs (forward peak at the opposite plant, tanh hold), cross the body, and jolt at every plant.
- `scripts/mech.gd`: `sprint_rate()` sets the AnimationPlayer `speed_scale` to speed / 20 (floor
  7 / 20) while `MECH_sprint` plays, so the ramp and brake no longer skate. Footfalls now use
  `MechSprint.LENGTH` instead of an assumed 1.0 s.
- `tests/test_mech_sprint.gd`: the clip tests were rewritten. They check the length and key count,
  that the floor contact moves at 20 m/s across the whole stance, contact vs flight, the bob and
  sway, the zero-z root, each arm's peak at the opposite plant, every key in reach, and the rate rule.
- `tests/_probe_mech_sprint.gd`: new poses, plus `screenshots/mech-sprint/cycle-side.png`, a
  12-frame strip of the whole cycle. It warms up 10 frames first; the old empty `contact-side` frame
  was a shot taken before the window had drawn.

Departure: the swing foot comes down with near-zero paw-back and stops dead (a stomp). The grill
asked for it to "arrive moving back at 20 m/s" and also for "the farthest point is the landing", and
in the mech's frame those two can't both be true.

Full suite: the 4 failures are the same pre-existing, unrelated ones (habitat owners, menu_refresh x2,
pixel_look). All sprint tests pass. Not yet played, uncommitted.

## GTA-style third-person camera

Spec: [specs/pilot9-gta-camera.md](../specs/pilot9-gta-camera.md). Grilled, specced and built the same day.
The ask: a camera identical to GTA or Red Dead; resolved to GTA V's on-foot camera, no auto-recenter.

- `scripts/pilot9.gd`: arm 3.5 -> 2.5 m, shoulder 0.4 -> 0.8 m, pivot raised 0.4 m (`camera_pivot_raise`)
  to orbit head height. `tilt_limit` is now `tilt_up_limit` 45 / `tilt_down_limit` 60. The stick gets a
  response curve and a full-deflection ramp (pure statics: `_look_stick_shape`, `_look_ramp_step`,
  `_look_ramp_factor`). `_handle_camera_state()` eases a 0.3 m pull-back for sprint/dash and a 0.4 m
  pivot drop for crouch/slide. Collision, FOV kicks, sway and dip are untouched.
- Tests: new `tests/test_pilot9_camera_gta.gd` (7); the shoulder and zoom tests were updated for the new
  numbers. Suite: 1029 tests green; the 3 range-dummy script errors were already there. Boots clean.
- Not played, and not seen on screen. The framing numbers are GTA V from memory, so expect to tune.
