  ring, no dot; reload dims the arms and refills right, bottom, left; hit marker kept. Drawn at the
  barrel's projected point (IK convergence distance), clamped to 60 px.
- Probe: settled aim, barrel within 0.01 deg of the IK line at camera pitch -13/0/+10, offset ~0.
  Not played.
