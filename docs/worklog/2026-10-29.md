## Arrival shot, revision 2 (refine pass)

The user asked for a refine pass that doesn't look AI-made: detail only with a reason, the building
properly on the ground, and the shot hit with our post and composite. Analysed a probe frame against
the reference, grilled nine decisions (spec `docs/specs/cutscene-arrival.md`, "Revision 2"), then
built them over nine probe passes. Final frame: `docs/specs/cutscene-arrival-r2.png`.

The base now sits on a stepped, buttressed foundation with snow aprons, footings and its real cast
shadow. Forest and snow follow the terrain (treeline, slope, ridges, gullies, clearings) through a
shared clear map that also carries a switchback road to a portal, a power-line cut and 18 avalanche
chutes. The ranges come in three layers with a massif, and the far tower stands on a ridge spur. The
roofs have jobs (landing pads + hangar, ops housings, utilities run), the five towers have roles
(comms, control, pad, exhaust with steam, radome), and the clouds are metaball heaps plus a level
cloud sea, with the sky streaks fixed. The haze is stronger and the arrival has its own CinemaPost.
I left the near band off and wrote down why in the spec. Arrival tests pass (12). Not yet played.
