## Intro space shot frame rate

User: the intro space shot's frame rate looks bad. Measured in a real window (2560x1440 render, RTX
3060, vsync off): 31 ms/frame (32 fps) from the planet onward, GPU-bound. The mega sky city was 18 ms of
it (every planet pixel: 4 supersamples x every shape, about 8 loops each), the star sky ~11 ms where it
fills the screen. Fixed, all exact except the sky's soft layers:

- City cell mask: `tools/gen_planet_city.py` now also writes `CITY_CELLS`, a 1-degree bit mask of where
  any shape, its shadow or a pixel's samples can reach (`CELL_ARC` / `CELL_TOWER_ARC` margins cover
  today's shadow offsets; raise them if those uniforms grow). `city_near()` skips the rest of the
  planet. Regenerating left the layout byte-identical.
- City per-pixel cull: `city_cull()` collects the shapes near the pixel once (bit sets in `CityCull`);
  every shape loop walks only those, in index order, so results match the full loops.
- Sky: star cells rejected on their first hash before the rest (same stars); wash / nebula / Milky Way /
  dust lanes moved to a half-res pass (`use_half_res_pass`, lanes ride in alpha), stars stay full res.

Stills with post off and the clock frozen are pixel-identical to before on the planet and city (only
the title fade frame differs run to run). Result: 15 ms GPU at t = 6 (city 3.7, clouds 4.2, the rest
1-2 ms each); full playback with vsync holds 60-63 fps through the planet with no frame over 18 ms, and
the stars-only opening runs at the 120 Hz cap. Suite: only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures.
Not yet played, uncommitted.

## Anime animation (stepped poses)

User: RETRO looks good; wants 24 fps anime-choppy character animation as another switch, game still
smooth. Grilled 7 decisions (all recommendations taken), docs/specs/anime-animation.md:
pose + whole-body rotation step, position smooth; 12 poses/s (on 2s); both pilots + range dummy;
own ANIMATION row (SMOOTH / ANIME, ANIME default) + U; a new move shows at once and restarts the hold;
mantle/vault/wall climb hold world position too; reflect spin on 1s (24) against the wagon wheel.

- `AnimeHold` (scripts/anime/anime_hold.gd), one per character, process_priority 1000. Shared 1/rate
  grid; steps on the grid, on a move-key change, or on a contact-flag flip. Holds a target node's
  global transform relative to the reference body's interpolated position (world space in contact
  moves), interpolation OFF on the target while engaged; SMOOTH restores rest local + INHERIT + reset.
- `AnimePoseHold` (scripts/anime/anime_pose_hold.gd): SkeletonModifier3D that keeps itself last on
  the skeleton, copies every bone pose on a step and writes it back between.
- pilot9: holds `character/Armature` (game logic reads `character`, so it is never touched); move key
  = tree state + dash + reflect + equip; contact = climbing/vaulting/wall climbing; 24/s while the
  reflect spins. Range dummy: new `Hold` node between it and `Visual`, `Visual` (recoil wobble) as an
  inner local.
- Settings: `anime_animation` row in graphics under LOOK; the user's settings.cfg set to anime.
- Tests: new tests/test_anime_hold.gd (10 tests: row/default, 12 per second at 60 Hz, 5-frame holds,
  move break restarts, 24 on the override, rotation held / position smooth, contact holds world,
  SMOOTH restore, inner locals, pose modifier, pilot9 wiring). Probe tests/_probe_anime_hold.gd on the
  real trial pilot: drawn head changes ~13 times/s in ANIME (first frame included), every frame in
  SMOOTH; headless boot logs nothing new. Suite: 1292 tests, only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship
  failures. Look and feel not judged headlessly. Uncommitted.
- Fix, same day. User: "something is fighting something, movement looks super blurry". The hold wrote
  the Armature's `global_transform`, which solves the local against the parent's physics-tick global;
  Godot's scene-tree interpolation draws a non-interpolated node at the parent's INTERPOLATED global x
  local, so the drawn model was off by up to a tick of motion and swung every physics tick (0.25 m at
  15 m/s on a >60 Hz screen). Now the local is solved against `get_global_transform_interpolated()`
  of the parent (refreshed before _process). New probe tests/_probe_anime_jitter.gd (moves pilot9 at
  15 m/s, measures drawn Armature vs drawn body): old code spread 0.249 m, fixed 0.0000 m. Suite: only
  the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures.
- Eye-strain pass, same day. User: not blurry any more but "jarring, it hurts my eyes". Explained the
  likely causes (held rotation snapping his silhouette where the eye tracks him, uneven rhythm,
  sampled in-betweens reading as dropped frames, contact moves stepping position) and the user took
  the recommendation: rotation smooth by default (pose only), steady beat (a new move shows at once
  but the next pose lands back on the shared beat), and live QA dials F1 rotation / F2 beat /
  F3 contact / F4 rate 12-15-24 with a 3 s readout, shared static vars on AnimeHold, not saved. Spec
  "Eye-strain pass". Tests: 13 in test_anime_hold.gd (+ smooth-turn default, steady beat, contact
  off); suite only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures; both probes unchanged (~12 poses/s,
  0 m drift). Uncommitted.
- User liked 24 best on the F4 dial -> `DEFAULT_RATE` 24 (clock tests pin 12 for themselves). But it

  given: uniform clock sampling of smooth clips is what low frame rate looks like, not what limited

  Options put to the user; no build yet. Suite: only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures.
- ANIME v2, same day. Grilled 8 decisions (all recommendations taken); spec rewritten
