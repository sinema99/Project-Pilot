# Worklog — 2026-09-28

**pilot9's afterimage dash lag — measured, specced, fixed and played the same day.** See
[specs/pilot9-dash.md](../specs/pilot9-dash.md), "Frozen skeletons, not baked meshes".

## What the user said

"our new after effect dash lags the game pretty hard." It lagged on every dash, not only the
first - which ruled out a first-use shader compile and pointed at per-ghost work.

After the play: "played it, no more lag." No adjustments.

## What was wrong

Each ghost was frozen with `MeshInstance3D.bake_mesh_from_current_skeleton_pose()`. pilot9 is
12 skinned meshes, about 58k vertices, and the bake reads every one back from the GPU, skins it
on the CPU and uploads a new mesh - **~27 ms per ghost**, six ghosts in a 0.25 s dash. Measured
windowed with `tests/_probe_dash_lag.gd`: dash frames averaged 12.5 ms with a 42 ms worst, against
5.5 / 6.1 ms for the same dash with MOVEMENT EFFECTS off.

## What shipped

A ghost is now a `Skeleton3D` of its own with his pose copied onto it, carrying copies of his
meshes that **share his mesh and skin**; the GPU skins it like it skins him. Prototyped first in
`tests/_probe_frozen_skeleton.gd` (0.03 ms to pose a ghost, no spikes), then built into
`scripts/pilot9_afterimage.gd`:

- **A pool** of `afterimage_max_count` ghosts, built at load and hidden. A dash shows them, a
  clear hides them; nothing is created or freed mid-dash. Rebuilt at a launch only if the meshes
  under `character` changed (a different held item) or the count was retuned.
- **The pose** is snapshotted on his skeleton's `skeleton_updated` - after the staff-hold and
  wall-hand modifiers - and only while a dash is live.
- **The launch ghost** waits for the first snapshot after the hard cut, instead of a drawn frame.

After: dash frames 5.56 ms average / 6.36 ms worst, the same as standing still; one drop 0.1 ms.
The look, spacing, colour and dissolve are unchanged. `pilot9.gd`'s calls are unchanged.

## Tests

799 pass (the headless run still segfaults at teardown after PASS - pre-existing). The launch
test uses a real snapshot instead of faking a drawn frame. Two new: a ghost's skeleton carries
his exact pose on his own mesh and skin, and a clear hands the ghosts back to the pool rather
than rebuilding it. Headless tests can now see real ghost poses, which the bake never allowed.

## Afterimages at 50% opacity

Ghost fill and ink now draw at `afterimage_opacity` (0.5, new export in the Afterimage group).
Two depth-only passes at render priority -1 go down first, so each ghost shows only its front
surface - no limbs or neighbouring ghosts showing through. Spec: docs/specs/pilot9-dash.md,
"The afterimages" point 5. Suite passes (799 tests). Not played yet.

Retuned to 0.2 the same day: 0.5 was still too solid and blocked the camera.

## Speed lasers replace the ankle ribbons

Grilled against a reference clip (a plane with white bolts shooting off behind it) and built.
Spec: docs/specs/pilot9-movement-vfx.md section 5 "Speed lasers", departures under "Built".

- The two ankle ribbons are gone: code, every `vfx_wind_*` dial, `_wind_feet()`, their test.
- Bolts fire from a ring round his chest, 0.35 m behind him, and fly back in a cone flaring
  15-20 deg - empty in the middle, so from the chase cam they point in at him from the screen
  edges and pass beside the camera. A bolt aimed within 0.6 m of the lens is re-rolled.
- Each bolt: a white spindle that stretches as it flies, ~0.25 s, stays in the world once fired.
  ~6/s and 1.5 m at 8.5 m/s up to ~25/s and 4 m at 20 m/s. Same on/off rules as the wind; the
  dash stops new bolts but lets live ones finish. Tuning: the `vfx_laser_*` exports.
- Departure: CPU bolts in an ImmediateMesh rather than GPU particles, so the tests can see them.
