## Cockpit storm: snow revision

Spec: [specs/cutscene-cockpit-storm.md](../specs/cutscene-cockpit-storm.md), "Revision 2026-10-16: snow".
Grilled with the user ("the snow is just going straight back ... all the flakes are uniform"). Only the
flakes in `shaders/cockpit/snow_storm.gdshader` changed; sky bands, cloud bands, lightning, colours, cruise
speed and the vanishing point are as before. Seat, pilot, holos, camera untouched.

- **Wind field** (`wind()`): S-lanes (`lane_bend` 0.22), finer gust shove (`turbulence` 0.12), and eddies on
  a 9-round grid drifting down the stream at `eddy_drift` 0.45 (slower than the flakes, so they pass
  through) that rotate the field round their heart (`eddy_curl` 1.7 rad, chance `eddy_chance` 0.3 calm ->
  0.7 storm, stronger with gusts). It warps (phi, log angle) before the flake cells, so it is a field fixed
  on the sphere and flakes follow curved paths; tails follow the warped lane.
- **Flakes**: no rim or capsule. Four layers (140/90/54/30 cells round), specks + 16% lumpy tumbling clumps
  + 6% big tufts on the near layer, per-flake flutter, own tail length and lean. Far layers step toward
  the sky colour (cel, 4 steps). A leading-edge shade (`flake_shade`) keeps them readable on the pale calm
  (white washed out without it); it fades into the sky as the storm comes in.
- **Patchy density**: two wrapped noises drifting with the stream scale density 0.15x-1.9x; calm base
  density 0.22 -> 0.32 (the calm was too thin once patchy).
- **Skimmers** (`skimmers()`): 20 slots of 1.3-2.2 s lives, each life alive or not by the storm at its
  birth (`ramp` uniform, set by `CockpitSnow.set_ramp` from the driver), mean 5 calm -> 15 storm. Hit
  (grow in 0.06 of life), squash flat, creep, accelerating curved slide toward the vanishing point with
  stretch, shrink away; 30% break into three bits after half-life. Fixed size on the glass
  (`skimmer_size` 0.03 rad).
- Probe-checked (calm 0.5 s, ramp 5.5 s, storm 7.2 s, `tests/_probe_cockpit_storm_shot.gd`): an eddy
  curl and a skimmer slide read in the storm still. Motion itself is not checked. Suite: 1222 tests,
  only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures. Not yet played, uncommitted.
