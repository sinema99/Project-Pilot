# 2026-10-18

## Descent: composite look

Spec: [specs/descent-composite.md](../specs/descent-composite.md). Grilled with the user from a descent frame
("it looks flat") against an online raw-render vs final-composite breakdown of a cel-shaded shot.

- **`DepthComposite`** (`scripts/rendering/depth_composite.gd`): a new general `@tool` CompositorEffect,
  post-transparent, HDR before CinemaPost and the tonemap. It reads the depth buffer (distance through
  the inverse projection, which checked out in the debug view). Its passes: far pixels into a quarter-size
  buffer (with haze and light on), 3 blur passes, then a composite in place: near band darkened, tinted and
  lifted, far hazed toward the light, the light added (HDR, so env glow blooms it), the blurred background
  wrapped over everything nearer. `debug_view` 1-3 shows distance bands / the dark mask / the wrap buffer.
- **Descent** carries `[DepthComposite, CinemaPost]` on its WorldEnvironment. `DescentShot.light_px`
  (1240, 340) places the light; `apply(t)` pushes the lightning `flash` into the composite and the shafts.
- **Shafts** (`DescentShafts`, `shaders/cutscene/descent_shaft.gdshader`): 5 cards from `DescentShot.SHAFTS`,
  tops 40 m above the deck edge, feet on the ground at 3-4.8 km, soft edges + ends + depth fade, 9 s breath.
- Probe-tuned (scratch probe on the `_probe_descent_shot.gd` pattern, 1.5 s / breach flash / 15 s / bolt).
  First pass blew the gap into a white disc and the snow went one flat blue. Retuned: light 0.55 HDR at
  radius 0.16, near band ramps over 15-400 m so the snow gradients dark-to-light toward the horizon, tint
  desaturated, haze 0.5, shafts 0.5. Flash lifted the ruin too; now it only lifts beyond 12 m.
- Suite: 1265 tests, only the 2 known test_ichi_parked_ship failures. The "material is null" errors from
  test_intro_movie happen on clean HEAD too. Not yet played, uncommitted.
